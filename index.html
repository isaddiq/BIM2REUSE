BIM2REUSE

From BIM to Circular Construction

BIM2REUSE is a free, browser-based circular construction platform. Import an IFC model to explore its geometry and semantics, check which elements carry the material and property data reuse decisions need, and save the project locally. It then builds a material inventory, component passports, a reuse assessment, a deconstruction plan, a material bank, optimised reuse matching for a new building, and a carbon and cost comparison, each traceable to the IFC element it came from. Files are parsed on your device with web-ifc, so nothing is uploaded.
